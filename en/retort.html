Brick Retort

Era 1Farm

Saltpeter/salt/acetate + sulfuric → nitric/hydrochloric/acetic; cinnabar → mercury (no acid). Fire.

Runs on fire. Acid salt + 500 mB sulfuric acid → acid: saltpeter → 350 mB nitric, salt → 500 mB hydrochloric, calcium acetate → 500 mB acetic. Cinnabar — no acid, roasting → 125 mB mercury (for mercury fulminate in primers). Cinnabar drops when mining redstone ore (pickaxe 25 %, more with Fortune, the excavator too).
