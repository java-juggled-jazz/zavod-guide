A 9x9 square around itself, twice as fast. Boom sections on top: up to 3, 21x21, power x1.5 per section.

The heavy excavator (steel): a 9x9 square around itself, twice as fast per block, 32 hp.

  • Boom sections stacked on top: each adds 2 blocks on every side — 9 → 13 → 17 → 21x21 (3 sections max).
  • Power ×1.5 per section: 32 / 48 / 72 / 108 hp. Blocks per second — same as without a boom.
  • Everything else is like the bronze one: hopper, hatches, backfill (Shift + empty hand), redstone pause, the column under the machine stays.

Assembly

Shift + empty hand — dig / backfill. Redstone — pause.

  1. The excavator digs a 9x9 square around itself layer by layer, leaving its own column.

  2. Boom sections stacked on top (up to 3, 2 here): +4 to the square each, power x1.5.

  3. Unloading hatch right next to it, face into a chest or a cart.

  4. Drive: a pulley beside it, shaft to a steam engine.
