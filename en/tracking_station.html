Satellite console: reports per mode (Shift — mode), EU per request. An empty map in hand — a report sheet with a picture: satellites, weather, reconnaissance (area image, forts, oil, raid).

The tracking station is a satellite receiving console. Place it, feed it IC2 EU (up to MV), click with an empty hand — the report of the current mode, Shift + empty hand — next mode (satellites, weather, reconnaissance, telemetry). Every request costs EU (reconnaissance — 2000). Without the right satellite in orbit the mode stays silent.

Report sheet: click with an EMPTY vanilla map in hand — instead of chat text you get a sheet with the mode's picture (the map is used up, EU as for a report; one legend line in chat). The sheet has no words — icons and digits; the map name is "Reconnaissance, day N". Make a copy in the crafting table (sheet + empty map), hang it in an item frame on the wall.

  • Satellites: a row per type (navigation, weather, reconnaissance, communications), a bar per satellite — days until deorbit (red — 3 or less).
  • Weather: the next 20 minutes (one game day): day/night, rain, thunderstorm; a tick mark with a number — change in that many minutes; hatching — unknown beyond that (after a change the weather holds at least 12000 ticks — that much is certain).
  • Reconnaissance: an orbital image (color by biome — sea, desert, forest, mountains, snow), the station in the center, north up. Red square — a fort with a garrison (troop count next to it), grey — cleared; black circle with a white rim — oil (bigger — richer), yellow rim — gas cap; red arrow — where a raid will come from, at its tail "nights until the raid/how many" (0 — tonight). Scale is automatic: the smallest (1–16 blocks per pixel) that shows everything in the report; the bar in the corner — blocks per segment.
  • Telemetry: no sheet — chat only.

A map with a cross comes from the seismic station: an empty map in the inventory + a survey — a cross on the deposit under your feet, otherwise on the nearest one.
