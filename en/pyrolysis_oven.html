Wood Distillation Retort

Dry distillation: 64 logs -> 64 charcoal + 2560 mB wood gas + 1280 mB creosote + 6400 mB pyroligneous acid in 5 minutes. Sections in a row to the sides, up to 3. Kindling (not logs) only for drying the first charge, then it runs on its own gas; keeps 1000 mB of gas for itself.

Assembly

Sections between two heads belong to neither. Breaking a section loses its charge.

  1. The head retort facing you: loading, unloading, firebox, tanks.

  2. Sections side by side along the face (left and/or right), up to 3 — 3 here: each one is one more charge.
