Steam Excavator

Digs a 5x5 square around itself down to bedrock. Boom sections on top: up to 4, 13x13, power x1.5 per section. Shift + empty hand — dig/backfill; hatches adjacent; redstone — pause.

The steam excavator (bronze) digs a 5x5 square around itself — layer by layer, from the machine's level down to bedrock. It leaves the column right under the machine alone: that is what it stands on. Driven by a shaft through a pulley.

  • Boom sections go in a stack on top of the excavator. Each one widens the square by a block on every side: 5 → 7 → 9 → 11 → 13x13 (4 sections max, a fifth and above do nothing).
  • Power ×1.5 per section: 16 hp without a boom, 24 / 36 / 54 / 81 hp with 1–4 sections. Blocks per second stay the same as the bare excavator — a wider layer just takes longer.
  • Added or removed a section — the excavator restarts its pass from the top (it will finish what it skipped).
  • The dig goes into a hopper, from there into adjacent unloading hatches (all / useful / spoil) or pipes.
  • Shift + empty hand — dig / backfill (rock from the bottom up, dirt on top). Redstone — pause.
  • Water is bailed out, lava stops it. Chests, machines, shafts and bedrock are left alone.
  • The old BuildCraft quarry is gone: large areas are dug only with the excavator.

Assembly

Shift + empty hand — dig / backfill. Redstone — pause.

  1. The excavator digs a 5x5 square around itself layer by layer, leaving its own column.

  2. Boom sections stacked on top (up to 4, 2 here): +2 to the square each, power x1.5.

  3. Unloading hatch right next to it, face into a chest or a cart.

  4. Drive: a pulley beside it, shaft to a steam engine.
