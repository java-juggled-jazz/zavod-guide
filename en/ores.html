engineer's handbookРусский

Ore comes in veins, not scattered: one big vein per dozens of chunks, with rare small pockets around it (“traces”). Found a pocket — a vein may be close. Geologist kit: click stone for a sample of the cube around it (how much ore, not where). Oil — signs show in the sample, the seismic station finds the trap. Depth is Y on F3. Climate runs in bands along Z: +Z hotter (deserts, jungles, savannas), −Z colder. Recipes are in NEI.

No vanilla scatter: Coal Ore, Iron Ore, Gold Ore, Redstone Ore, Diamond Ore — veins only.
