Launch Vehicle

Era 5Atom and space

A rocket on RC reinforced concrete: kerosene + Linde oxygen (175 %) or nitric acid (270 %). The amounts depend on the payload, click with an empty hand; ±10 %, otherwise a crash or burnt satellites.

Place it on Railcraft reinforced concrete (the launch pad). Right-click — up to 3 satellites. Fuel by pipe: kerosene (Shukhov column) and an oxidizer — liquid oxygen from the Linde unit or nitric acid. How much — click with an empty hand: the rocket needs 1000 mB of kerosene + per satellite by orbit height (reconnaissance 500, weather 700, navigation 800, communications 1500); oxygen 175 % of the kerosene, nitric acid 270 %. Tolerance ±10 %. Any tank underfilled — the engine dies at ~25 blocks and the rocket falls on the pad: the blast breaks blocks, scrap flies. Overfilled — it flew off and the satellites burnt up. Launch — a redstone signal, a 10 s countdown, step back. Place the block on reinforced concrete — a rocket (~7 blocks) stands on the pad. Every launch is a new rocket from the Carpenter. The flight is visible: the rocket lifts off slowly and speeds up, from 60 blocks it pitches over along its course, at 150 the first stage separates (falls off to the side and blows up without breaking blocks), at 320 it is gone above the clouds — "in orbit" or "burnt up". The roar carries ~160 blocks.
