Alumina + coke → aluminium; 8 copper/nickel dust + vitriol/sulfur → 8 ingots + slime; 8 blanks → 8 graphite; 4000 water → 25 mB heavy water. EU, HV.

Electrolytic cell on EU (up to 512 EU/t, HV). Aluminium: 2 alumina + 1 coke (the anode burns away) → an ingot for 50 000 EU. Refining: 8 copper dust + copper vitriol or 8 nickel dust + sulfur → 8 ingots for 8 000 EU. Anode slime goes to a separate output: 40 % of a copper batch, 60 % of a nickel batch (fractions accumulate, no randomness) — platinum is made from the slime. Graphitization (Acheson): 8 carbon blanks (3 petroleum coke + bitumen in a crafting table → 3) → 8 graphite for 80 000 EU. Heavy water (Vemork): input slot empty, ≥ 4000 water in the tank → 25 mB of heavy water for 10 000 EU; pipe the water in and the heavy water out.
