Tree Feller

Era 2Steam and mechanics
idle
running

Fells trees in the 5×5 square in front of its face, a log every 2 s. Axe in the slot (1 durability per log). Saplings by hand. Shaft 4 hp.

Face it to the woodlot: it cuts the 5×5 square right in front of its face, from its own level 32 blocks up, top down, a log every 2 s at full speed. Any axe goes in the slot: 1 durability per log, a broken axe stops the saw. Takes anything that drops as a log: vanilla, 2×2 dark oak, Forestry trees of any girth. Leaves are left alone (they decay and drop saplings, Forestry ones with genes), saplings are not planted — that is by hand. Logs go to the chest behind, else below: then by hopper into the sawmill or the pyrolysis oven. Shaft 4 hp. A plank or log building in the zone gets cut too.
