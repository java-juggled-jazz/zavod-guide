Mixer

Era 2Steam and mechanics
idle
running

Mixes two fluids: nitrating mixture, aqua regia, IC2 coolant. A small 1 hp shaft or a hand crank (Shift + empty hand), by hand twice as slow.

The mixer is a lead-lined vat with a stirrer. It mixes two fluids with no heat, pressure or steam:

  • nitric 500 + sulfuric 500 → nitrating mixture 1000 (2 s) — for TNT, picric acid, nitrocellulose;
  • nitric 250 + hydrochloric 750 → aqua regia 1000 (2 s) — to dissolve gold;
  • distillate 1000 + methanol 250 or bioethanol 250 → IC2 coolant 1000 (5 s).

This used to be the chemical reactor's job — those recipes are gone from it.

  • Drive: a small shaft (1 hp) through a pulley — full speed. No shaft — hand crank: Shift + empty hand, one turn = 20 ticks of work at half speed (up to 100 stored). Nitrating mixture by hand — 4 turns.
  • Tanks: two inputs and an output, 4000 mB each. The second tank only accepts a fluid that pairs with the first — you cannot clog the vat with something incompatible. Take the product from the output by pipe or bucket.
  • It holds acids (the lining); for acid storage use a lined barrel.
