Evaporator

Era 2Steam and mechanics
idle
running

Brine → salt, lye → potash, water → distillate, mash → alcohol + stillage; up to 3 effects in a row.

Evaporates a solution with heating steam: seawater — salt, lye — potash, fresh water — distillate. Put units in a row, each facing the next: steam boiled off the first heats the second and third — three times less steam per batch.

Alcohol: ferment wort in a barrel (water + sugar, wheat or potatoes, close it with Shift + empty hand, wait a day), pour the mash into the evaporator. 1000 mash give 150 alcohol (bioethanol) and 1 stillage (mulch — compost, into the saltpeter bed). Alcohol, like distillate, drains into a tank or pipe under the unit. The output tank holds one fluid: drain the distillate before the mash.
