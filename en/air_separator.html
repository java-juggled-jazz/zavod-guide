Linde Air Separation Plant

Era 4Oil and electricity at scale
idle
running

Blower + water -> nitrogen 3000 + liquid oxygen 1000 mB (for the rocket).

Needs a blower right next to it (on a shaft) and water. One batch: 10 blast cycles + 500 water -> 3000 nitrogen + 1000 mB of liquid oxygen. Both go out by pipe: nitrogen into ammonia (chemical reactor), oxygen is the launch vehicle's oxidizer. When the tanks are full the surplus is vented, work does not stop.
