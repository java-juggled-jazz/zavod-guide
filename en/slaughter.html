Slaughter Machine

Era 2Steam and mechanics
idle
running

A knife on a crank driven by a shaft (2 hp): kills an adult farm animal in the stall right in front of its face, a stroke every 5 s. Leaves babies, tamed and name-tagged animals alone. Picks up meat, leather and bones and hands them to a chest behind or below. Fence the stall: one block, entered from the fattening pen.
